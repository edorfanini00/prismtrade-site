SMS Privacy Policy

How Celeritech International Corp handles information for the Celeritech Hermes Alerts text-message program.

Effective date: September 5, 2026

1. Who we are

This SMS program, Celeritech Hermes Alerts, is operated by Celeritech International Corp, 1200 Brickell Avenue, Suite 310, Miami, FL 33131 ("Celeritech", "we", "us"). Messages are sent from +1 360 300 5673.

2. Information we collect

When you enroll in and use the program, we collect:

  • Mobile number — the phone number from which you text START (or that you provide when opting in).
  • Name — the name you provide, if any, when enrolling or that is associated with your Celeritech staff account.
  • Message content — the text of messages you send to the Celeritech Hermes assistant and the alerts and replies we send to you, along with the date, time, and delivery status of those messages.
  • Consent records — the date, time, and method (web form or START keyword) of your opt-in and any opt-out.

The web enrollment form on this site does not transmit or store any data; enrollment is completed when you text START from your phone.

3. How we use it

We use this information only to:

  • deliver operational alerts (task and status updates, reminders, system notices) that you requested from the Celeritech Hermes assistant;
  • respond to your own text requests to the assistant;
  • send required program messages (opt-in confirmation, HELP responses, opt-out confirmation);
  • maintain records of consent and comply with carrier, legal, and regulatory requirements.

We do not use this program for marketing, promotions, or advertising.

4. No sharing for marketing — ever

Your mobile opt-in data and SMS consent will not be shared with, sold to, or rented to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information collected through this program is not shared with any third party for their own use.

We share information only with service providers that help us deliver the messages — such as our SMS messaging platform (Twilio) and the wireless carriers — and only to the extent needed to send and receive messages and to maintain compliance records. These providers are permitted to use the information solely to provide services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our staff.

5. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies depending on the alerts you request and the requests you send. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

6. Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP (or CANCEL, QUIT, END, UNSUBSCRIBE) to +1 360 300 5673 at any time to stop receiving messages. We will send one final message confirming your opt-out and will not send further messages unless you re-enroll by texting START.
  • Reply HELP at any time for program information, or email info@celeritech.biz.

7. Data retention

We retain your mobile number, name, and consent records for as long as you are enrolled in the program and for up to four (4) years after you opt out, to document consent and satisfy carrier and legal record-keeping requirements. Message content is retained for up to twelve (12) months for operational and troubleshooting purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymized, unless a longer period is required by law. Opt-out (STOP) records are retained indefinitely so that we do not message you again.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information described in this policy. Access is limited to Celeritech personnel and service providers who need it to operate the program.

9. Your choices and rights

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your program information by emailing info@celeritech.biz. We will honor such requests subject to our record-keeping obligations described above.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
