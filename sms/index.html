Celeritech Hermes Alerts

Operational text-message alerts and replies from the Celeritech Hermes assistant, for Celeritech staff who ask for them.

About this program

Celeritech Hermes Alerts is operated by Celeritech International Corp. It is intended solely for Celeritech staff members who request text alerts from the Celeritech Hermes assistant, an internal operations assistant.

Messages are operational alerts — task and status updates, reminders, and system notices — and replies to the staff member's own text requests. Volume is low. This program sends no marketing, no promotions, no lending offers, and no age-restricted content.

Messages are sent from +1 360 300 5673.
