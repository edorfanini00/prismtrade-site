Terms for the Celeritech Hermes Alerts text-message program.

Effective date: September 5, 2026

1. Program name and description

Celeritech Hermes Alerts is an SMS program operated by Celeritech International Corp ("Celeritech", "we", "us") from the number +1 360 300 5673. It delivers operational alerts — task and status updates, reminders, and system notices — and replies to a staff member's own text requests, from the Celeritech Hermes assistant, an internal operations assistant. The program is intended for Celeritech staff members who request these alerts. It does not send marketing, promotional, lending, or age-restricted content.

2. How to opt in

You can enroll in either of two ways:

  1. Web form: complete the enrollment form at https://helios.prismtrade.co/sms/, check the consent box, and then text START to +1 360 300 5673 from your mobile number to complete enrollment.
  2. Keyword: text START to +1 360 300 5673 from your mobile phone.

By enrolling, you agree to receive operational alert and reply text messages from Celeritech International Corp (Celeritech Hermes Alerts) at the mobile number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase or employment.

3. Confirmation message

After you opt in, you will receive the following confirmation message:

Celeritech Hermes Alerts: You are now subscribed to operational alerts and replies from the Celeritech Hermes assistant. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Privacy: https://helios.prismtrade.co/sms/privacy/

4. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on the alerts you request and the messages you send to the assistant. Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details about your plan.

5. How to opt out

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP, CANCEL, QUIT, END, or UNSUBSCRIBE to +1 360 300 5673. After you send one of these keywords, you will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed:

Celeritech Hermes Alerts: You have been unsubscribed and will receive no further messages. Text START to re-subscribe. For help email info@celeritech.biz.

After that, no further messages will be sent unless you re-enroll by texting START.

6. How to get help

Reply HELP to +1 360 300 5673 at any time. You will receive this response:

Celeritech Hermes Alerts: For help email info@celeritech.biz. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel.

You can also email info@celeritech.biz.
